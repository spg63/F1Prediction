Assumptions and checks

  • Current-season recency-weighted form baseline; driver/team components are confounded descriptive adjustments, not identified causal skill.
  • Season-level parameter draws and team/weekend shocks represent heuristic model uncertainty; Monte Carlo standard errors measure simulation noise only.
  • Future sessions assume full distance and unchanged latest known entrants. Cancellation, shortened races, future substitutions, weather forecasts, strategies and unannounced penalties are not modeled.
  • Qualifying results require start+90min before cutoff; races start+180min and sprints start+60min. Published grid overrides qualifying once.
  • Retirement timing uses normalized 60/20-lap distances; late retirees can classify at floor(90% of winner distance). Finishing-order ranks include unclassified starters at the back.
  • 2026 countback: GP places, then GP qualifying places; residual exact ties receive random simulation allocation, not an FIA determination.
  • WARNING: 2 historical qualifying sources list fewer entrants than GP sources. Nonparticipation is possible; absent qualifying ranks are not imputed and countback coverage may be incomplete.
  • 2026 regulation reset: no constructor or driver pace estimates transferred from earlier seasons.

Sources, timing and rules: source review. See model assumptions and chronological backtest. Full rank distributions: championship.csv, races.csv. Zero estimates are Monte Carlo zeros, not mathematical impossibility; with zero hits, the approximate 95% upper sampling bound is 3/N.
